# World View

> World View (World View Enterprises) is a Tucson, Arizona stratospheric balloon company focused on remote sensing and persistent intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR). Acquired by Ondas Holdings in 2026 and operating as part of Ondas Sentinel, it flies uncrewed high-altitude balloons carrying EO/FMV, IR, SAR, and hyperspectral sensors for defense, government, and commercial missions. World View publishes no public developer program or API.
